Free Online QR Code Scanner

Scan any QR code straight from your browser — no app to install. Point your camera at a QR code, or upload an image of one, and the link or text is decoded instantly. Everything runs on your device, so your camera feed and images are never uploaded.

How to scan a QR code

Using your camera

  1. Allow camera access when prompted.
  2. Point the camera at the QR code and hold steady inside the frame.
  3. The result opens automatically — tap Open to visit a link, or copy the text.

From a saved image or screenshot

  1. Tap the photo button (bottom right).
  2. Choose an image that contains a QR code.
  3. The QR code is decoded from the image instantly.

Works on iPhone (Safari), Android (Chrome), and desktop browsers.

Frequently asked questions

Is this QR code scanner free?
Yes. It's completely free with no sign-up.
Do I need to install an app?
No. It runs in your browser. You can optionally install it as a PWA for offline use.
Are my images or scanned data uploaded to a server?
No. Decoding happens locally in your browser. Your camera feed and images are not uploaded.
Can I scan a QR code from a saved image or screenshot?
Yes. Use the photo button to pick an image and it will be decoded.
Does it work on iPhone, Android, and desktop?
Yes, in any modern browser.
Does it work offline?
Yes. After your first visit it's cached as a PWA and works offline.
